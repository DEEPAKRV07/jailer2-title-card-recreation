# Reference scene analysis

All 925 video frames decoded to 480x270 RGB; per-frame luma, bright coverage, centroid and absolute frame change saved. Visually inspected 0.5-second contact sheets plus 0.1-second samples from 15–18 seconds. Original timestamp-matched larger frames inspected in comparison sheets. No reference pixels used in renderer.

Stage boundaries are visual estimates, generally +/-0.1–0.3 seconds, not recovered authoring keyframes. The 3.766667-second field cut is detected at frame 113 in the every-frame difference trace. Rates/easing/depth descriptions are qualitative observations, not measured optical flow.

## Shared visual language

Deep black environment, white/silver luminous points and trails, high-contrast portrait, locally blooming glints. Text uses a jewel grid with cool highlights. Rain predominantly travels vertically; fragments swirl laterally and through apparent depth. Portrait motion settles rather than freezing abruptly. Exact camera transforms and particle velocities cannot be recovered from these flattened references.
