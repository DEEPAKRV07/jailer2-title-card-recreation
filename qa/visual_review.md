# Visual review record

## Inspected evidence

The two original videos were decoded for every-frame metrics, and their dense sheets were visually inspected. The generated v1, v2 and v3 stills were inspected, including a reference/generated comparison. Final `reviewed` beginning/midpoint/end frames were inspected in `portrait_reveal_comparison.jpg`. Native 3840 × 2160 letter and portrait PNGs were inspected before rendering the master. After encoding, `encoded_master_contact_sheet.jpg` was inspected.

This records visual inspection, not user approval or an exact visual-match certification. Files bearing the earlier working tag `approved` were an internal naming choice; no user approval is implied, and these redundant files are not included in the ZIP.

## Iteration

1. Initial v1: title and K too dim, early rain too faint, face left of reference, too many fine assembly particles.
2. Corrected jewel brightness and size, added larger foreground stars, shifted the portrait anchor right, reduced assembly-particle count from 5200 to 2800, raised trail/head luminance, and lowered broad portrait luminance with deeper blacks.
3. Further brightened vertical trails and increased head size; added elongated bright drops. Retained restrained bloom and stable source-texture detail. The revised K and full KARTHIKEYAN name are legible inside the safe width.
4. Native 4K inspection: recognisable supplied face, glasses, hairstyle and clothing remain; no rectangular photographic boundary visible. The portrait retains the source's pose. Letter texture is a regular faceted grid rather than a pasted S image.
5. Encoded-master inspection: expected stage order present; no garbled title text or rectangular picture edges in inspected samples. Black margins remain clean. Beginning/middle/end portrait samples show scattered streaks, fragmented face formation and a settled portrait, respectively.

## Remaining differences

- Early rain is finer and more evenly spread than the reference's clustered, thicker highlights. Mid-reveal has more fine detail. This remains an approximation of the reference particle choreography.
- The supplied still already contains digital texture and rain. Its subject texture is preserved within the mask, with added procedural light/particles; it is not a recovered unprocessed photograph.
- Face angle, shoulder breadth and clothing differ from the reference person; the source is uniformly scaled, never stretched to force a match.
- Jeweled text is a 2D analytic faceted pattern, and the opening star uses 2D shaded triangles. Neither recreates the original unknown 3D scene or optical bloom exactly.
- KARTHIKEYAN is longer than the reference word, so it auto-fits to a lower relative cap height. Only the specified letter/name are displayed. The main name holds through otherwise empty later credit slots.
- The original full-screen flash is softened. The opening star-to-letter near-black pause and the terminal fade are intentional.
- Low-resolution previews and native renders use the same procedural rules; exact samples differ because texture sampling and source-resampling occur at their render resolution.

## Audio correction

The supplied extended AAC decoded to a stereo peak of 1.167706. A first 0.8-gain encode still produced three samples above full scale due to encoding overshoot. The delivered version uses gain 0.65 and was remuxed without video re-encoding. Final decoded peak: 0.878892; zero samples above full scale. The zero-offset correlations against the supplied audio exceed 0.998 in both channels. Content and timing are retained; loudness is reduced by approximately 3.74 dB.

## Verification boundary

All 654 master frames were decoded successfully and measured. Selected frames were visually inspected, not every frame by eye. No claim is made that every motion trajectory matches the reference or that a similarity score establishes quality. See the attached comparison for the actual visual differences.
